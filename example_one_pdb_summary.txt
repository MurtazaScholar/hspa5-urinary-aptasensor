Interpretation:
- nt_motif / aa_motif: nucleotide and amino-acid motif windows
- combo: motif-length pairing such as nt1_aa2 or nt3_aa1
- min_distance: minimum heavy-atom distance between the two motif windows
- interaction: 1 if min_distance <= 12 Å, else sampled non-contact negative
- interaction_score: distance-derived strength score for positives
- ss_motif: aptamer secondary structure labels aligned to nt_motif
